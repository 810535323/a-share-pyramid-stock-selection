# Industry Chain Valuation, Expectation-Gap & Trap Rules

Use different analytical rulers for different layers of an A-share industry chain. Never evaluate heavy-asset manufacturers, high-barrier tech suppliers, and cyclical price-takers with the same PE multiple.

## 1. Per-Layer Core Rulers

| Chain Layer | Economic Driver | Primary Fundamental Metrics | Primary Valuation Ruler | Classic Trap to Flag |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upstream (Equipment / Core Materials / Scarce Resources)** | High tech/resource barriers, long customer qualification, high switching cost | Domestic substitution rate (sweet spot 10%–40%), contract liabilities, single-quarter revenue acceleration, gross margin level (35%–60%) & trend | **Deducted PE (扣非真实 PE)** + PEG | **Headline PE Illusion**: Equity-investment or fair-value gains make headline PE look like 40–50x when real deducted PE exceeds 100x+ and PB > 10x. |
| **Midstream (Foundry / Manufacturing / OSAT / Heavy Processing)** | Fixed-asset operating leverage vs. heavy depreciation cycles | **Capacity utilization** (breakout inflection around 90%–95%+), ASP/wafer price trend, QoQ gross margin expansion, future CAPEX/depreciation schedule | **PB (市净率)** + Deducted PE | **Full-Capacity Peak Trap**: Buying at 100% utilization + high PB (> 5–6x) right before a massive new-fab depreciation wave or demand plateau. |
| **Downstream — Cyclical / Commodity-Linked (Memory, Chemicals, Metals, Shipping)** | Spot & contract product price cycle | Spot/contract price trend, inventory days, gross margin swing | **Price Cycle Stage + PB / Replacement Cost** (NOT headline PE!) | **Cycle-Peak Low-PE Trap**: Ultra-low PE (6–15x) + explosive YoY profit (+500%–2000%) often marks the *top* of a price cycle. When spot prices stall, margins collapse and low PE explodes upward. (Historical 2026 case: storage leaders fell ~76% from July highs despite single-digit/low-teen PEs). |
| **Downstream — High-Barrier Growth / AI & Edge Chips / Brands** | Product-cycle ramp, ecosystem lock-in, terminal pricing power | Single-quarter revenue acceleration, R&D-to-revenue discipline, gross margin resilience (45%+), major-client adoption | Deducted PE + Forward Growth Visibility | **Priced-for-Perfection Risk**: 120–160x PE stocks have near-zero margin of error; any single-quarter miss or supply bottleneck triggers violent multiple compression. |

## 2. Expectation Gap & Profit-Reallocation Analysis

When explaining why one layer rallies while another stalls or sells off:
1. **Check Consensus Shift (预期差)**: Markets re-rate the layer that moves from *pessimistic consensus -> verifiable beat* (e.g., midstream foundries flipping from "overcapacity fear" to "97%–100% utilization + 10%–20% price hikes + 2027 visibility").
2. **Trace the Profit Transfer ("Cake-Cutting Effect")**: A price hike in Layer A is direct margin expansion for Layer A, but **cost inflation / margin squeeze** for Layer B if Layer B lacks terminal pricing power.
3. **Map Forward Transmission**: Sustained high utilization and cash flow in Midstream subsequently validates future CAPEX orders for Upstream tool/material makers once Upstream digests high valuation and crowding.

## 3. Extraordinary Price-Volume Verification ("不平凡的量价关系")

Never rely on subjective "feeling" that capital is supporting a stock. Check three objective signals:
- **Sell-Off Stress Test**: On days when the benchmark index drops `> 1.5%`, compare target drawdown vs. index and sector ETF. True institutional accumulation shows *narrow sideways consolidation, shrinking volume on pullbacks, and leading rebounds on stabilization*.
- **Position Filter (Low/Mid Base vs. Climax Top)**: Resilience after a long consolidation or moderate base = accumulation. Resilience after a vertical parabolic run = potential distribution/catch-up crash risk.
- **Board & Lot Practicality Filter**:
  - `688` STAR Market: 500k RMB + 2y permission, 200-share minimum lot (verify single-lot cash cost against user's position limit).
  - `300` ChiNext: 100k RMB + 2y permission, 100-share lot.
  - `600/601/603/000/002` Mainboard: No extra threshold, 100-share lot.
  - **ETF Proxy Rule**: When individual leaders exceed a user's permission or single-lot cap, use the purest sub-sector ETF and monitor its **Top-10 constituent stocks** to judge true ETF entry timing.
